How to Use AgriHelp Mini

பயன்பாட்டு வழிகாட்டி

1. Select Your Cultivar & Stage: Use the top selector to pick your crop (Rice, Cotton, Sugarcane, Ragi, Coconut, Groundnut) and its current growth phase.
2. Simulate Local Climate: In the Advisory Hub, slide temperature, soil moisture, humidity, and pH to simulate today's weather or extreme forecasts.
3. Check Soil NPK Lab: Switch to Tab 3 to check fertilizer dosage tailored to regional soil types (Thanjavur alluvial, Coimbatore black, Madurai red loam).
4. Manage Field Tasks: Check off daily duties in Tab 4, guided by weather recommendations.
