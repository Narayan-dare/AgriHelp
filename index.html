Agronomic Fertilizer Dosage / பரிந்துரைக்கப்படும் உர அளவு

Calculated for target yield of Rice per Acre

1 Acre (4047 sq.m) Basis
UREA (46% N) / யூரியா 1.2 Bags
58 kg / acre

Apply in 3 splits: 20 kg basal, 20 kg at active tillering (day 25), and 18 kg at panicle emergence (day 45).

DAP / SSP / மணிச்சத்து 0.6 Bags
30 kg / acre

Apply 100% full dose as basal application before transplanting or sowing.

MOP (Potash) / பொட்டாஷ் 0.5 Bags
25 kg / acre

Apply in 2 equal splits (basal & panicle initiation) to strengthen crop straw and grain weight.

Biofertilizer & Organic Carbon Plan

Apply 5 tonnes of Farm Yard Manure (FYM) or 2 tonnes of Vermicompost. Inoculate seedling roots with Azospirillum (2 kg/acre) & Phosphobacteria (2 kg/acre) mixed in 50 kg compost.

Micronutrient & Soil Health Protocol

Zinc is deficient (< 0.8 ppm). Apply Zinc Sulphate (ZnSO4) at 10 kg/acre as basal. Do not mix directly with DAP.

How to Use AgriHelp Mini

பயன்பாட்டு வழிகாட்டி

1. Select Your Cultivar & Stage: Use the top selector to pick your crop (Rice, Cotton, Sugarcane, Ragi, Coconut, Groundnut) and its current growth phase.
2. Simulate Local Climate: In the Advisory Hub, slide temperature, soil moisture, humidity, and pH to simulate today's weather or extreme forecasts.
3. Soil Report Adder & Analyzer: In Tab 3, upload or paste your soil test report, or click sample presets. The analyzer instantly calculates NPK health grades, exact fertilizer bags/kg per acre, and organic biofertilizers.
4. Manage Field Tasks: Check off daily duties in Tab 4, guided by weather recommendations.
